# Independent product experience review

**Verdict: PASS for the integrated Editorial Intelligence Studio direction.** The actual application now has a coherent editorial identity and a clear source-investigation journey. This is an engineering/design judgment based on rendered use, not a measured participant preference or claim of hackathon success.

The strongest improvement is the relationship between activity, interpretation, original language and human action. Scene 47 does not merely show a risk badge: the planned wheeled dolly is placed beside the permit's differently worded pressure-bearing-platform restriction, with the recorded Clocked contribution explicitly identified. Exact text receives a paper reading surface and a readable serif; its model fields sit behind a disclosure. The human next action has a distinct treatment without implying permission.

## Adversarial findings and repairs

Actual use exposed real weaknesses: the opening initially read the wrong compact projection; comparison initially assumed one document; diagnostics collapsed after rerender; generic recovery instructions appeared on an unsupported source import; three-digit numbers/four-character scene IDs collided; short screens buried the next-action route; mobile success feedback duplicated itself. These were repaired and the relevant interactions repeated. The screenshot record includes those intermediate states and the corrected evidence.

## Weaknesses that remain

Source authority and operational forms still require careful reading and sometimes substantial scrolling. Progressive disclosure reduces their initial footprint but cannot remove the underlying documentary responsibilities. Large action queues are correct and filterable, though they remain a register of individual source tasks rather than a richer saved-query system. Long originals intentionally occupy substantial space when expanded. Cambria's exact metrics are best matched on Windows; fallbacks maintain a serif hierarchy but are not pixel-identical. The approved design supports light mode; a separately designed dark mode was not added.

No formal usability study, screen-reader certification, physical-device testing or new live provider run was conducted. Original recorded evidence is sufficient for this presentation acceptance; fresh provider behavior and production authorization remain existing engine responsibilities. The new-product recovery case had zero model journals; the full automated suite covers journal-rich and uncertain recovery. These limits should remain visible in any later demonstration claims.

The resulting product is ready for the one coordinated owner acceptance procedure. Further iteration should follow observed coordinator use, rather than adding visual decoration or hiding source uncertainty.

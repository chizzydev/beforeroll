# BeforeRoll — judge demonstration & write-up contract (M3-S5)

## Thirty-second pitch

When filming permits arrive as prose, coordinators must connect each condition and signed variation to tomorrow's planned scenes. BeforeRoll uses Clocked Decide to interpret these scene-to-clause relationships, then presents verifiable original source passages and unresolved human checks. A narrow variation may cover one scene but not another, even when the activities sound similar. No probability is represented as actual filming clearance.

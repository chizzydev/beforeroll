## Thirty-second judge promise

> A film coordinator receives a location permit and a call sheet. The words in the permit rarely match the crew's shorthand, and a variation that authorizes one scene may not authorize the next. BeforeRoll uses Clocked Decide to link source conditions to scenes, distinguish written restrictions from potentially operative scene-specific grants, and turn them into inspectable, human-reviewed preflight findings. The original documents and evidence remain visible. BeforeRoll never pretends its AI is the film office.

## Core sponsor contribution

- **Clocked Decide (core, meaningful):** for each clause/scene, two typed `noul` decisions — activity applicability and whether an *issued source clause* states a grant for this specific scene. Clocked supplies the linguistic relationship; local code supplies citation, conservative status and integration.
- **Clocked Extract (previously integrated):** structured field reading with source offsets for basic issued permit terms. Row-boundary parsing remains deterministic due to an earlier model error; this is a safety choice, not a weakness concealed.
- **Do not claim:** automatic legal authorization, advanced amendment-precedence reasoning, complete clause extraction, calibrated model probability, production-ready document ingestion, public multiuser deployment, independent customer uptake, or U01/U02 live success before those tests.

## Failures found and repairs

### 1. False abstention on current historical-object work

**Reproduced live:** E's provider correctly returned consent, gloves and conservator, with exact source offsets. BeforeRoll's broad `historical|photograph` fence suppressed all three S701 candidates. It still returned S702's consent, so this was a selective application composition defect rather than an Extract failure.

**Fix:** `scopeCaution` now separates explicit unissued/proposal/request warnings from historical subject matter. A clearly named focal scene's current directive (`require`, `must`, `shall`, `mandatory`) keeps validated facts reviewable when no past/reference/narrative cue contradicts that reading. Other/mixed scene and unsigned/proposed scope still quarantine. The rule is generic and contains no campaign scene IDs, source hashes, expected phrases or precomputed actions. It is a safety fence, not an issuance or legal classifier.

**Retest:** the untouched E source/gold/provider receipts reproject to all four expected candidates with no additional provider call. F then passed on fresh wording, document structure and provider requests. New tests cover current artwork/exhibit requirements, past photographs, unsigned proposals and other-scene isolation. E's initial FAIL is retained in `E_PRESENT_PHOTO/result.json`; corrected evidence is in `E_PRESENT_PHOTO/post-repair/`.

### 2. Disconnected old source-check bookkeeping

**Observed:** after genuine discovery, the original source-task queue claimed its exact passages had never been sent to Clocked, requested another analysis and said no model finding was available. The journey likewise showed zero source checks despite full discovery evidence. This contradicted the real application journal and encouraged redundant paid analysis.

**Fix:** each exact current dossier source task now carries its checked `discoveryCheck`, response hash, relevance and routing/caution. Current discovery counts are explicit alongside unchanged legacy Decide-only counts. The queue no longer claims accepted discovery pairs were unsent; ambiguous/scoped cases receive documentary-investigation actions. Source-review work stays open. Journey progress recognizes returned discovery comparisons. The scene workbench labels this evidence as discovery, without fabricating an old journal entry or replacing human authority/conditions.

**Retest:** authenticated post-restart reads across all six productions show all 42 current pairs assessed, zero duplicate unsent-check actions, all 42 original human source reviews still open, and recorded suggestion reviews retained. Legacy completed checks remain zero for these fresh productions, accurately reflecting that the old separate run was not executed.

An additional offline failure guard ensures that returned Decide coverage cannot make a failed/incomplete Extract run appear complete in the journey: incomplete discovery retains its own outstanding evidence class and stops the analysis checkpoint.

### 3. Incomplete “complete” export

**Observed during source/receipt tracing:** lifecycle backup already contained discovery journals, but `/dossier/complete` copied only the old Decide journals into the supposedly complete evidence package.

**Fix:** the complete export now includes the checked current/historical `discoveryJournals` when present.

**Retest:** authenticated JSON/Markdown/complete exports for all six productions preserve tasks, quotes, history and unchanged raw discovery journal SHA. A fixed-as-of export's dossier hash matches the standalone dossier. Separate-copy lifecycle recovery of actual A evidence preserves original files, workspace/reviews, raw journals and execution fences. These checks used **zero provider calls**.

## Limits and next action

The pass covers six short, developer-authored fictional UTF-8 cases and their frozen expectations. It does not establish broad real-world document selection reliability, complex PDF/DOCX live behavior, arbitrary mixed-scene prose or signature authenticity. Uncalibrated relevance can miss obligations; ambiguous and negative pairs stay manual review. Current-directive/narrative fencing remains conservative and incomplete; unfamiliar temporal or cross-scene wording needs responsible reading. The first live E failure is evidence of that risk, not something to hide behind the final pass.

All final 27 expected candidates have exact spans and correct frozen scene associations; no unsupported candidates, unauthorized scope transfers, invented grants or fulfilled conditions were observed. The **initial three omissions** were application defects, not provider failures, and were fixed. Provider interpretation itself passed these particular literal tests; it should not be generalized beyond them.

The strongest supported product is now real, reusable **source-grounded coordinator investigation**, with concrete documentary/physical/personnel candidates and durable uncertainty, not autonomous permission adjudication. Recommended next engineering action is targeted expansion of semantic/scope regression coverage from independently sourced fictional formats and improvements to source-evidence reconciliation under revisions. No further paid calls are required merely to raise this campaign's count. Historical GUI issues and competition presentation remain separate work.

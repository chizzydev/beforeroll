## Project title
BeforeRoll — Know what the shot depends on

## Short summary
Clocked turns filming paperwork and planned scene activities into source-linked coordinator review tasks. BeforeRoll separates prerequisites, documentary claims and human verification—without granting filming clearance.

## Overview / audience / problem and solution
The crew can film inside an observatory archive. But the next shot asks an actor to unfold a borrowed atlas. Room access alone does not establish handling authority, and an email requesting wider access is not an issued amendment.

BeforeRoll helps location managers and production coordinators investigate that gap. Import original paperwork and a scene register, verify text, select whole documents/scenes and preview a bounded analysis. Clocked connects activity to documentary conditions, returns literal prerequisites and organizes documentary claims. BeforeRoll validates source identity/offsets and builds scene-specific work with exact original evidence, review history and an exportable handover.

In our fictional Last Light showcase, Scene31 receives handling-specific consent, protection and supervision tasks. Scene32's case-only interview retains shared venue personnel but does not inherit those handling tasks. The claimed variation, cited CS-9 instrument and unissued extension remain human investigations. A saved review records progress, never legal clearance or physical fulfillment.

## Clocked endpoints and composition
Decide supplies typed relevance/documentary-claim judgments for each selected scene/passage. Extract returns three reusable literal lists: written instruments, physical protections and on-site personnel. Fill organizes claimed document roles, references, verbatim scope and suggested investigation targets. The outputs change actual scene cards, documentary investigations, Work, History and evidence exports.

BeforeRoll checks original text, Unicode spans, request/response identities, source/scene contracts and durable journal integrity before projection. It preserves uncertain/unsupported work, requires human authority/fulfillment decisions and never auto-clears filming. In the same-receipt two-endpoint ablation, CS-9 was an unsupported prerequisite; adding Fill retains it as a referenced-instrument investigation and surfaces the separate unissued request. One Fill scope reading omits S32 for a shared condition; both independently supported venue tasks remain and the disagreement is visible.

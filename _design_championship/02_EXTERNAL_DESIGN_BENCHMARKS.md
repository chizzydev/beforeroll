# External design benchmarks

Research date: 26 October 2026. Public first-party pages, documentation and screenshots; no private accounts or proprietary assets copied into prototypes. Descriptions below separate what was observed from what BeforeRoll should infer. Marketing imagery establishes visual references, not successful use of the authenticated application.

## Precision and restraint

**Linear.** Opened the [My Issues documentation](https://linear.app/docs/my-issues) and enlarged its actual product screenshot. [Captured reference](evidence/linear-issues.jpg). Observed a stable quiet sidebar, compact rows, a stronger selected row, state-group headings and sparse colored status marks. The documentation explains curated priority order and assigned/created/subscribed/activity tabs. Interpretation: hierarchy can come from ordering and typography instead of containers. Apply this to the scene register and outstanding work; avoid shrinking exact quotations to issue-row density. Linear's screenshot does not prove BeforeRoll's audience wants developer-style keyboard workflows.

**Vercel / Geist.** Inspected the [Geist system](https://vercel.com/geist/introduction), its public component directory and [design resources](https://vercel.com/design). [Captured reference](evidence/geist-system.jpg). Observed coordinated foundations, explicit theme controls and components for errors, empty states, destructive confirmation, pagination and copyable identities. Interpretation: premium quality includes consistent edge states. Borrow the discipline of named tokens and focus/error behaviors; do not import Vercel's grid aesthetic or make production documents resemble developer infrastructure.

**Raycast.** Reviewed its [public product page](https://www.raycast.com/), including the keyboard-led examples and extension actions. Observed a task-oriented launcher proposition rather than an accumulation of dashboards. Interpretation: a secondary command/search surface can speed expert navigation, but BeforeRoll's ordinary scene/source/action flow must remain visible. Research was page/documentation level; no desktop Raycast installation or measured latency test was performed.

**Notion.** Read the [wiki guidance](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/category/wiki) and its navigation to pages, database views, search and permissions. Observed one content system exposed through several task views. Interpretation: scenes and work can be views of the same production, not competing data structures. Do not introduce editable generic blocks that could detach source quotations from byte identities. This was documentation research, not an authenticated Notion usability test.

## Film-production mental models

**Frame.io.** Inspected the [review and approval page](https://frame.io/features/review-and-approval), with embedded interface screenshots. [Captured reference](evidence/frame-review.jpg). The shown viewer keeps comments associated with the reviewed media; the page explains anchored feedback, precise viewer controls and configurable metadata. Interpretation: exact source selection should remain visible while recording a review; a source revision should have a clear version context. BeforeRoll must not borrow an approval label that implies permit validity. No Frame.io account or live review was created.

**StudioBinder.** Inspected [production software](https://www.studiobinder.com/film-production-software/) and first-party stripboard guidance found during research. Screenshots: [production presentation](evidence/studiobinder-workflow.jpg), [shoot section](evidence/studiobinder-callsheets.jpg). Observed domain stages from writing through shooting and scenes/schedules as central working objects. Stripboard guidance describes scene details, list/board views and direct script context. Interpretation: scene numbers, locations and dates orient a coordinator more naturally than task hashes. Keep BeforeRoll's narrower documentary intelligence purpose; do not pretend to replace a full scheduling system. Some site media did not play in this browser; no authenticated schedule editing was tested.

## Evidence and document intelligence

**Harvey Vault.** Opened the [Vault product page](https://www.harvey.ai/platform/vault) and [getting-started documentation](https://help.harvey.ai/articles/getting-started-with-harvey). [Captured interface image](evidence/harvey-vault.jpg). Observed a quiet document table and contextual query workspace, with file/source choices near the question. The docs describe source-linked answers and distinguish general Q&A from a dedicated redline workflow. Interpretation: original source and selected context must be first-class; AI prose alone is insufficient evidence. BeforeRoll should give revisions an explicit impact workflow. A cookie overlay partly obscured the marketing screenshot; no private Vault, claimed extraction accuracy or quantified efficiency result was independently validated or borrowed.

## Cinematic editorial language

**BFI / Sight and Sound.** Opened [Sight and Sound](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound), inspected its opening and story/archives hierarchy. [Captured reference](evidence/bfi-editorial.jpg). Observed publication identity, large editorial headlines, smaller category/author metadata and clear current-versus-archive sections. Interpretation: filmmaking identity can come from type, sequence and careful reading rather than a clapperboard icon. BeforeRoll should put current source beside its reading guide, keep historical evidence clearly labeled, and use generous quotation measure. Do not borrow magazine artwork, masthead or article layouts; an operational decision needs shorter routes than an editorial website.

## Benchmark translated into criteria

| Benchmark principle | Concrete test in BeforeRoll |
|---|---|
| Linear's restrained density | Can scenes and tasks be scanned without every row becoming a card? |
| Geist's completeness | Are loading, failure, stale-state and recovery states designed, not improvised? |
| Raycast's task orientation | Is the next action named with a verb and available in context? |
| Notion's coherent objects | Do alternate views retain the same scene/source identity? |
| Frame.io's anchored review | Is the exact quotation visible at the human decision? |
| StudioBinder's production vocabulary | Can a coordinator orient by scene, location, day and activity? |
| Harvey's documentary context | Can a reviewer inspect original source and revision before relying on a summary? |
| Editorial hierarchy | Is there a clear reading order without losing operational speed? |

The proposed winner combines these principles in an original scene-first editorial workspace. It copies no reference screen or asset. Reference quality was evaluated critically: large marketing heroes, small gray metadata, opaque approval language and generic AI prompt boxes are not automatically suitable here.

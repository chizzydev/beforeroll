# Design championship protocol

Registered 26 October 2026 before prototype construction and scoring. Single expert assessment; no claims of independent participant research, measured comprehension or competition prediction.

Weights: Clocked comprehension 20; usability 20; craftsmanship 20; cohesion 15; identity 10; film credibility 5; responsive/accessibility 5; implementation 5. Score each 0–10; weighted total = sum(score × weight)/10. A failed evidence boundary or unusable essential journey eliminates regardless of total.

Common material: frozen U01 Archive Floor, Scenes 47/48, four original clauses, genuine older-contract recordings. No rescore or new provider call. Preserve complete source and raw outputs behind disclosure. Label design-authored summaries separately from model output. U02 remains the baseline secondary-case check.

Common states: first opening; S47 restriction discovery; S47 addendum versus S48 unsigned request; production work; exact evidence depth. Desktop 1440×960, narrow 390×844. Check finalists also at 768×1024. Same task order for each: open discovery, find quotation, compare exception, locate next human action, inspect provenance, return to work. Screen switches are comparison controls, not proposed app navigation.

Finalists get interactive source selection, scene switching, exact-source review with required rationale, staged preview/commit, persistent prototype-only review, document/history navigation, revision-impact storyboard and export of design notes. The sandbox must clearly distinguish simulated human work from original recorded model evidence.

Record click counts from a defined first state, observed obstacles and screenshots. Do not invent five-second/30-second participant success rates or elapsed task times. Assess those as expert predictions and state uncertainty. Review all six before ranking; refine finalists once for observed defects, then rescore.

# Six complete design theses

All six use frozen U01: the exact original Archive permit, Scenes 47/48, and genuine older-contract Clocked outputs. No synthetic confidence or new provider response. Shared content is deliberate experimental control. Each has five directly addressable browser states: opening, discovery, exception, workflow and depth. A gray comparison toolbar sits outside the proposed product design.

The prototypes use local system font stacks so the actual typography can be evaluated offline. No remote font substitution is assumed. Common raw evidence comes from `prototypes/case-data.js`, a read-only projection of the existing fixture/recording; original sources are unchanged. All summaries authored for the design study are labeled as reading guides, separate from recorded output.

## A · Cinematic Editorial

**Identity:** an intelligent production reading desk. Film relevance comes from scene numbering, considered typography and documentary comparison rather than film props. Warm limestone, carbon, rust action and quiet sage intelligence surfaces. Georgia for headlines/quotations; Segoe UI for controls and ordinary prose. Serif indicates a reading hierarchy, not extra legal authority.

**Architecture:** a production brief opens on one specific scene question. Four destinations—Production, Scenes, Work, Documents & history—retain context. The discovery spread puts planned activity and Clocked's relationship on the left, original language and human next action on the right. A scoped exception comparison is a purposeful transition, not another warning panel. A work register and evidence reader extend the same editorial frame.

**Interaction:** investigate Scene 47 → read restriction → compare AD-4 and the request → select Scene 48 → record a reasoned exact-source review → verify → apply → reopen history. Source provenance is a disclosure within a readable original, not inline noise. Advanced review sessions and revision preview retain distinct confirmation steps.

**First impression:** “The shot is planned. Is the paperwork?” appears beside the dolly/platform example. This is a working example, not a marketing hero with unrelated art. **Discovery:** all four semantic roles are visible at 1440×960 after refinement. **Exception:** parallel document-language panels, with no green “approved” scene. **Work:** a concise row register and capability index. **Depth:** a selected paragraph reader with exact raw fields and full source available.

**Density/responsive:** two reading columns at wide sizes; sequential reading below 760px; context-preserving action jump on narrow screens. Risks: long quotations can grow vertically, editorial headings can waste space, and unrestrained prose can make the product feel like a report. The design contract caps headline scale, uses operational rows and never forces a full brief before ordinary work.

## B · Precision Intelligence Studio

**Identity:** a serious dark intelligence workstation. Graphite `#15191f`, layered slate, pale blue action and warm focus ring. Segoe UI throughout; restrained mono only for raw identities. No lime, grids or cinematic glow.

**Architecture:** the scene register remains a compact rail; selected scene content occupies an instrument-like workspace. Planned activity sits above paired model/source panes, with an action bar immediately after. The opening offers a concise proposition and a real workspace preview. Work and document readers retain the same compact dark frame.

**Interaction:** high-frequency scene switching without leaving the register. Source disclosure, review preview and history stay part of the selected investigation. Source and model layers have different surfaces, not interchangeable cards. A complete action register supports batch-oriented experts without making metrics the product.

**Five states:** opening workspace snapshot; rail-led discovery; two scoped exception panels; row-based work register; selected documentary reader. **Responsive:** rail becomes a horizontal scene selector; paired panes stack; source identity fields wrap. Risks: too close to established professional SaaS, dark fatigue for long documents, and a dense center pane that can make provenance feel technical again. It is the strongest workstation alternative, with less distinctive film identity than A.

**Prototype:** `?concept=studio`; [opening](evidence/studio-final-opening-1440.jpg), [discovery](evidence/studio-final-discovery-1440.jpg), [narrow](evidence/studio-final-discovery-390.jpg).

## C · Documentary Evidence Lens

**Identity:** a calm forensic comparison instrument. Porcelain, navy ink, cobalt action and pale blue interpretation surface. Segoe UI headlines and controls; Georgia for exact source passages. The main graphic language is alignment and connective structure.

**Architecture:** equal visual access to intended activity and documentary text, with a central Clocked bridge. The opening shows those two vocabularies as the product demonstration. Source comparison is the primary mental model; production work is the follow-through. This is not chat-first or a general project dashboard.

**Interaction:** select scene and paragraph → inspect relationship → compare scoped exception → choose human action. The model's middle column is subordinate to the original documents. A direct evidence reader supports raw returns and history without filling every comparison cell with metadata.

**Five states:** paired intention/condition opening; three-column discovery; issued-wording/request comparison; work register; exact paragraph reader. **Responsive:** two documentary columns at tablet sizes, central relationship below; one ordered column on narrow screens. Risks: the center column can become a long text bottleneck; multiple long originals would need a different comparison mode. This is exceptionally clear for the thesis but less ownable as a film-production identity than A.

**Prototype:** `?concept=lens`; [opening](evidence/lens-final-opening-1440.jpg), [discovery](evidence/lens-final-discovery-1440.jpg), [narrow](evidence/lens-final-discovery-390.jpg).

## D · Modern Production Command

**Identity:** a production day board. Cool paper, navy masthead, blue action and restrained time typography. Segoe UI gives operational precision; source quotations use Georgia. No numerical risk score or KPI-card wall.

**Architecture:** the shooting day and unresolved work are primary. The opening juxtaposes the product proposition and a day callboard. Scene discovery has an action rail; evidence continues in the main reading column. Work has the strongest dedicated day view in the field.

**Interaction:** select the next operational concern → investigate its scene → verify source → return to the day's work. Date/time/scene are first-class; detailed documentary history remains accessible through a source destination. The same review transaction still applies.

**Five states:** day-led opening; action-rail discovery; exception comparison; dated work board; evidence reader. **Responsive:** day work precedes detail; the action rail follows evidence on narrow screens. Risks: telling a coordinator what to do before showing why can understate Clocked; could be mistaken for scheduling software. Strong support view, weaker central competition story.

**Prototype:** `?concept=command`; [opening](evidence/command-opening-1440.jpg), [discovery](evidence/command-discovery-1440.jpg), [work](evidence/command-workflow-1440.jpg).

## E · Film Production Atelier

**Identity:** a contemporary production folio. Oat paper, olive-brass action, carbon text, broad margins and restrained stacked-page treatment. Georgia headline/scene numbers; practical sans controls. No distressed textures, imitation handwriting, stamps or clapperboards.

**Architecture:** original production documents occupy a folio page; model interpretation and coordinator action live in the margin. A folio index organizes source intake, reviews, revisions and export. The original has visual primacy over the machine.

**Interaction:** browse the folio → read a marked passage → consult the coordinator margin → attach a review. The issued/request comparison becomes facing folio pages. Work is presented as the working register inside the folio rather than a separate dashboard.

**Five states:** asymmetrical folio opening; source page/margin discovery; facing exceptions; work folio/index; original source reader. **Responsive:** margin notes become the following reading section; the page shadow is reduced in importance. Risks: users must cross the spread to follow plan → model → evidence → action, and paper metaphors can suggest an artificial document authority. Distinctive, but less effortless than the best finalists.

**Prototype:** `?concept=atelier`; [opening](evidence/atelier-opening-1440.jpg), [discovery](evidence/atelier-discovery-1440.jpg), [work](evidence/atelier-workflow-1440.jpg).

## F · Radical Clarity

**Identity:** a quiet decision companion. White, near-black, rare rust emphasis and careful single-column measure. Segoe UI titles; Georgia quotations. No decoration beyond alignment and a thin reading thread.

**Architecture:** one meaningful question at a time. Discovery is a sequential narrative rather than simultaneous panes. The production work list stays direct; the full capability index is disclosed below it. The source reader keeps advanced evidence available.

**Interaction:** follow one scene concern down a reading thread → record the review → return to the next concern. It minimizes choices while investigating but makes cross-document comparison a separate state. Human review remains exact-source, not a yes/no clearance wizard.

**Five states:** question-led opening; sequential discovery; parallel exception state; minimal work register; traceable source reader. **Responsive:** natural one-column reading, with less layout change than other concepts. Risks: hides the simultaneous relationship that makes Clocked memorable, delays the source at desktop sizes, and makes capabilities feel secondary. Excellent restraint, insufficient whole-product presence as the champion.

**Prototype:** `?concept=clarity`; [opening](evidence/clarity-opening-1440.jpg), [discovery](evidence/clarity-discovery-1440.jpg), [work](evidence/clarity-workflow-1440.jpg).

## What is common and what actually differs

Evidence, source wording, model returns, comparison tasks and the minimum interaction contract are held constant. That prevents a more favorable story or invented model result from winning. The opening composition, dominant object, discovery structure, source/model adjacency, density, navigation inside the workspace, typography and film identity differ. Shared review forms and evidence disclosures enforce the same transaction across alternatives; they are not six complete implementations of the existing backend.

All six are navigable. The A/B/C finalists received deeper review-persistence and provenance/revision interaction validation. Actual file import, billable analysis, encrypted recovery and source mutation remain the accepted app's responsibilities, represented honestly as prototype navigation/storyboards where relevant.

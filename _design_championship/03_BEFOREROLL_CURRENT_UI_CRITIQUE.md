## Suspected weaknesses checked against evidence

Confirmed: monospaced kickers and letter spacing; uppercase across many headings/actions; repeated status pills and bordered rounded panels; persistent dotted background; lime action/state/number accents competing for attention; technical enum labels and raw identities in ordinary work; repeated non-authority warnings; a sidebar that becomes empty while the main column continues through long forms; duplicated work through scene actions and the complete action queue.

Not confirmed as a universal fault: body text is mostly sans serif, not entirely monospace. Useful responsive rules and accessible form labels already exist. The original-source and historical evidence controls are functional rather than decorative. The current introduction is clearer than the earliest closure screenshot; evaluating an obsolete screenshot alone would be misleading. The recorded-versus-live distinction is a product strength to retain.

The application's problem is chiefly **presentation architecture**, not absence of capability. The current seven workflow checkpoints correspond to real lifecycle operations, but expose internal state transitions before the user has understood one scene concern. Do not remove those operations. Rehouse them under four stable task destinations: Production, Scenes, Work, Documents & history, with contextual advanced actions.

## The product experience to protect

An intended dolly shot may be relevant to a restriction expressed as “pressure-bearing wheeled camera platforms.” Clocked identifies that relationship. The original passage supplies documentary language. Scene 47's addendum names conditions; Scene 48's unsigned request does not establish an extension. A human investigates source scope and records next work.

This should read as **planned activity → Clocked intelligence → documentary evidence → human action**. Model probabilities must never become legal authority, green clearance badges or progress percentages. A changed source must retire affected evidence, not silently repaint it current. A recorded result must never become a newly run result through typography.

Design-only exploration has not changed application source, evidence originals, storage, authentication, billing or the accepted recovery flow. Zero new live Clocked requests.
